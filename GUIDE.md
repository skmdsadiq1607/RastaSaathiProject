## 1. Getting Started (Sign Up & Login)

To use the full capabilities of the Rasta-Saathi emergency grid, you need to create an account.
- **Register:** Click on the "Register" or "Sign Up" button on the homepage. Enter your full name, phone number, and a secure password. 
- **Login:** If you already have an account, enter your registered phone number and password to access your dashboard.

## 2. Setting Up Your Profile

Your profile acts as your central identity card during emergencies. Keeping it updated is crucial for rapid response.
- Navigate to the **Profile** section from the main menu.
- **Language Preferences:** Select your preferred language. The AI Medic and system alerts will communicate in this language.
- **Emergency Contacts:** Add up to 5 emergency contacts (Family, Friends). When you trigger an SOS, these contacts are automatically notified via SMS/WhatsApp with your live GPS location.
- Ensure your phone number is correct as emergency responders will use this to contact you.

## 3. The Main Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is your primary emergency command center.
- **Map View:** At the top of the dashboard, you will see a live Google Map pinpointing your current GPS location.
- **Emergency Types:** You can select the type of emergency (e.g., Accident, Medical Emergency, Fire) and provide additional text context (e.g., "Two-wheeler collision on main road").
- **SOS Button:** A prominent, pulse-animated RED button is positioned at the center of the screen.

## 4. Triggering an Emergency SOS

When a crisis occurs, time is of the essence. Here is exactly what happens when you trigger an SOS:

1. **Activate:** Press the large **SOS Button** on your Dashboard. 
2. **GPS Lock:** The system instantly captures your exact live coordinates.
3. **Automated Dispatch:** 
   - **Nearest Ambulance:** The system scans the database for the closest available ambulance hub and dispatches it immediately.
   - **Trauma Center:** The nearest hospital capable of handling your specific emergency is alerted and reserved.
   - **Police Precinct:** The local police station is notified of the incident.
4. **Live Map Updates:** Your dashboard map will immediately update to show:
   - Your location (Red `EMERGENCY SITE` marker)
   - The dispatched Ambulance (Orange `Activity` marker)
   - The target Hospital (Blue `Hospital` marker)
   - The nearest Police stations.
5. **Tactical Dispatch Panel:** On your screen, you will see the estimated time of arrival (ETA) for your ambulance and the names of the assigned hospital and police precinct.

## 5. AI Medic & First Aid Guidance

While waiting for the ambulance to arrive, the **AI Medic Advisory** kicks in instantly on your dashboard.
- Based on the type of emergency you selected, the AI will provide step-by-step, critical first-aid instructions (e.g., "Apply pressure to the wound", "Do not move the neck").
- The instructions are displayed in clear, actionable bullet points, highlighting critical warnings in red or yellow.
- It will communicate in the language you selected in your profile.

## 6. Downloading the Official Incident Report (PDF)

Once an SOS is triggered, the system generates a highly detailed, cryptographically secure receipt of the incident.
- Click the **"Download Report"** button located on the active SOS status bar on your dashboard.
- This downloads a premium A4 PDF to your device.
- **What’s inside the PDF?**
  - **Victim Details:** Name, Contact, and precise GPS coordinates.
  - **Tactical Dispatches:** The assigned Ambulance name/ETA, the target Hospital, and the Police precinct.
  - **Custom Notes:** Any messages you typed before hitting SOS.
  - **AI Medic Logs:** A complete transcript of the first-aid instructions provided to you.

## 7. Viewing SOS History & Past Receipts

You can review all past emergencies handled through your account.
- Navigate to the **Profile** page and scroll down to the **Incident History** section.
- Here, you will see a list of all your past SOS triggers, including their date, time, and resolution status.
- You can click the **Download Report** button next to any past incident to retrieve its historical PDF receipt.

## 8. Road Safety Guidelines

Rasta-Saathi is not just for emergencies; it's also about prevention.
- Navigate to the **Road Safety** section from the main navigation menu.
- Here, you can read curated, interactive guides on defensive driving, handling vehicle breakdowns, helmet laws, and pedestrian safety.
